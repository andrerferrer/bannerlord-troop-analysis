## Interpretation

- Elite Archer is the highest-rate Mallister terminal branch in both contexts. In field battles it combines the best Mallister kill rate with low observed losses.
- House Guard is the most durable terminal branch in field battles (4.1% casualties), but its kill rate is lower than Elite Archer and Eagle Knight.
- Eagle Knight is a credible second offensive branch, especially in siege attack, but its intervals overlap the other terminal branches; the data do not establish a precise ordering between all three with high confidence.
- The ordinary Knight, Horseman, Archer, Man at Arms, and Footman lines remain below the independent-battle gate in at least one context. Their diagnostic rates must not be promoted into reliable claims.

## Decision

The Mallister family test can close for the three terminal branches in field and siege attack. The evidence supports Mallister as a strong, well-rounded castle package, led by Elite Archer rather than House Guard. It does not support calling Mallister the best overall package: compatible field data still place Ravens' Teeth far above every Mallister branch, and siege comparisons remain observational.

No frozen model is changed or recalibrated by this batch.
