# Workstreams

## Data quality

P1 review, context review, correction replay, validation, and canonical dataset generation.

## Identity

Alias governance, track-aware troop-ID mapping, and verified empirical-feature joins.

## Descriptive analysis

Five-battle rankings, uncertainty, context splits, insufficient-evidence reports, and stability analysis.

## Data collection

Additional siege attack, siege defense, and controlled experiment observations.

## Mechanistic analysis

Weapon speed, damage, reach, armor, shield, skill, ammunition, and perk experiments.

## Modeling

Battle-grouped explanatory prediction, feature ablations, residual analysis, and eventual calibrated simulation.
